## Farewell, Bike Bot

As of **November 2025**, **Bike Bot** has officially stopped providing service.

When Bike Bot was first created, the goal was simple: build something useful, reliable, and genuinely fun for Discord communities. Over time, it grew beyond expectations, and seeing people use and enjoy it made the project incredibly rewarding.

However, running a bot like Bike Bot is not just about writing code.

---

## 再見了，Bike Bot

自 **2025 年 11 月起**，**Bike Bot 已正式停止提供服務**。

當初開發 Bike Bot 的初衷很單純——打造一個實用、穩定、並且能真正為 Discord 社群帶來價值的機器人。隨著時間推移，這個專案的成長超出了預期，而看到大家使用並喜歡它，是一件非常值得的事。

但維持這樣的服務，遠不只是寫程式這麼簡單。

---

## Why the service was shut down

To keep Bike Bot online, an instance had to be running **24/7** on a hosting platform. This came with ongoing costs and maintenance responsibilities, including:

- Continuous hosting fees  
- Monitoring uptime and crashes  
- Maintaining dependencies and updates  
- Adapting to Discord platform changes  

For a personal project, the **cost and effort required were no longer sustainable**.

After careful consideration, the decision was made to shut down the service.

---

## 為什麼停止服務？

為了讓 Bike Bot 持續運作，需要在主機上 **全年無休（24/7）地執行程式**，這帶來了不少長期負擔，包括：

- 穩定的主機租用費用  
- 監控服務狀態與錯誤  
- 維護套件與版本更新  
- 因應 Discord 平台的變動  

對於一個個人專案而言，這樣的 **時間與金錢成本已無法長期負擔**。

因此，在審慎評估後，最終決定終止 Bike Bot 的服務。

---

## What this means

- Bike Bot will no longer respond to commands  
- Hosted instances have been shut down  
- The project may be archived in the future  

Thank you to everyone who supported, tested, and used Bike Bot. Your feedback and enthusiasm made this project meaningful.

---

## 這代表什麼？

- Bike Bot 將不再回應任何指令  
- 所有官方託管的執行個體已關閉  
- 專案未來可能會進入封存狀態  

衷心感謝所有使用、測試與支持 Bike Bot 的人。正是因為你們，這個專案才值得被完成。
